How to Identify Fake Respondents in Real Time

Spotting fraudulent survey entries early requires watching for patterns that genuine respondents rarely produce. Response time anomalies are one of the clearest signals; a survey completed in seconds almost certainly wasn't read. Selection of the same column in all rows is termed straight-lining and shows signs of disengagement or automatic answers. Mismatch of geo-data is an indication that a respondent’s geographical location is not consistent with his/her IP address and is very important in collecting information from verified survey respondents in California because location is central to the entire research design. The pattern of duplicate IPs shows fraudulent activity, whereas open-end gibberish shows bots and reward-seekers.

Survey Fraud Prevention Strategies

For prevention of fraud among online panels, an approach involving multiple layers is essential as compared to just using one filter. Start with pre-screening surveys that screen out non-qualified participants even before the actual survey is conducted. Use digital fingerprinting techniques in order to catch duplicate entries despite cookie deletions and browser changes. Include attention checks and instructions to click on a certain response to immediately catch careless respondents.

At the panel level, consistent panel screening methods and behavioural audits remove chronic bad actors before they corrupt new studies.
