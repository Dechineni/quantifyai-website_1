What Does "Verified" Actually Mean in Survey Research?


What does respondent verification actually involve? The concept essentially involves ensuring that the respondent really is what they claim they are, and works within the discipline or profession they claim to belong to, and meets the criteria set by the researcher for them to contribute to the data analysis process. Generally, respondent validation entails identity, professional, and attention/consistency checks within the survey process. Without this scrutiny, sample authenticity becomes a guessing game, and decisions end up resting on responses that may not reflect real market behavior.
